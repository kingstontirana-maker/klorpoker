TEXAS HOLD’EM · NO LIMIT

Hand rankings

From strongest to weakest. Your best five-card combination from your 2 cards and the 5 on the board counts.

    Main rules

    • Blinds. The player left of the dealer posts the small blind, the next one the big blind. Heads-up, the dealer posts the small blind and acts first before the flop.
    • Rounds. Preflop → Flop (3 cards) → Turn (1) → River (1). A card is burned before each round.
    • Bets. The minimum bet is one big blind. The minimum raise equals the last full raise. All-in is always allowed.
    • Incomplete all-in. It does not reopen the betting for players who have already acted, unless several all-ins together make a full raise.
    • Side pots. An all-in player can only win up to the amount they covered; the rest forms a side pot.
    • Showdown. The last aggressor on the river shows first, otherwise the first player left of the dealer. A losing hand may be mucked, except when there is an all-in.
    • Ties. The pot is split; an odd cent goes to the first winner left of the dealer.
    • Joining a game. When you sit down or come back from sitting out while a game is running, you post a big blind, unless you are in the blinds.
    • Rake. No flop, no drop. Uncalled bets are returned without rake.
